## 🔬 Research & Academic Work

**Thesis Concept — *Pendulum Fusion: A Paradox Beyond the Clock***
> A theoretical framework exploring what emerges when classical, relativistic, and quantum pendulum models are unified into a single system. The project investigates the paradoxes and physical implications that arise at the boundary between Newtonian mechanics and relativistic dynamics.
>
> *Group research project · University of the Punjab*

**Research Interests**
